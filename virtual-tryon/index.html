📷 Get a better result

Use a sharp full-body image, stand naturally, keep your outfit visible, and avoid strong shadows. For hats and accessories, a clear photo of your head and upper body can work better.

🔒 Photo privacy

This page previews uploads locally and does not store them. AI generation sends both images to FASHN. FASHN says base64 input copies are used for processing and deleted when the request finishes, with a one-day cleanup backstop. Request records are retained. Read FASHN's data policy ↗

AI connection settings Worker setup required
The AI key stays on the Worker, not in this website.

To activate AI, deploy the included Worker code and add FASHN_API_KEY and APP_ACCESS_CODE as Worker secrets. Never paste your FASHN key here or into GitHub. AI generations use paid credits; ask a parent or guardian before setting up a paid account or sending a personal photo to an external AI service. Setup steps are in the project README.
