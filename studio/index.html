01 / The studio

Curiosity, made tangible.

Engineering is a process of asking better questions, testing ideas and refining the details until the parts work together.

A hands-on practice built around making, learning and solving.

FarisTech X is the personal engineering and maker brand of Faris Ahmed. The work spans electronic circuits, PCB design, microcontrollers, robotics, 3D modelling and physical prototyping.

Each project starts with a clear problem or idea, then moves through design, experimentation and iteration. The focus is on understanding how things work — and building something useful from that understanding.

Build with intent. Test with curiosity. Improve with every iteration.
